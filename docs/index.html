01 Overview

ABSTRACT · COMPANION SUMMARY

Solving inverse partial differential equation (PDE) problems is a fundamental topic in scientific research due to its broad significance across a wide range of real-world applications. This survey organizes AI methods into three closely related tasks: inverse problems, inverse design, and control.

We connect methodological paradigms with scientific applications and discuss physics-informed architectures, limited real-world data, uncertainty quantification, and inverse foundation models. This companion brings together a searchable method archive, literature-reported results, and a matrix of representative workflows.

02 A unified view of inverse PDE problems

The same physical system gives rise to three different questions. What caused an observation? Which design achieves a target? Which action steers a system?
